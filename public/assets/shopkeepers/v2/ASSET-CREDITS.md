# CartGain v2 shopkeeper art

Merchant-selected cartoon humanoid-robot shopkeepers supplied by the project owner:

| Variant | Character sheet | Expression sheet | Persona |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Morgan | `morgan-character.png` | `morgan-expressions.png` | Friendly |
| Riley | `riley-character.png` | `riley-expressions.png` | Playful |
| Alex | `alex-character.png` | `alex-expressions.png` | Strict |

`CHARACTER-SPECS.md` is the owner-supplied generation brief and contains the
shared style block, silhouette rules, expression grid, and servo-smooth
humanoid-robot acting notes. Character cards were described by the owner but
were not included in the supplied files.

The images are owner-supplied assets. Record formal ownership or commercial-use
permission here before redistributing them independently. They are rendered from
the application's own static asset path; no third-party CDN or runtime image
service is used.
