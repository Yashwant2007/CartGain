# CartGain VRM shopkeeper art

Merchant-selected VRM shopkeepers supplied by the project owner:

| Variant | Model | Animation pack | Persona |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Meera | `meera.vrm` | `clips_meera.glb` | Warm |
| Kabir | `kabir.vrm` | `clips_kabir.glb` | Calm |
| Verma | `verma.vrm` | `clips_verma.glb` | Playful |

The models are VRM 1.0 files with 39 humanoid bones and preset expressions. The clip packs carry 30 named animations shared by all three rigs. They are owner-supplied placeholder assets and are rendered from the application's own static asset path; no third-party CDN or runtime asset service is used.
