## 1. Positioning & competitor angle

**The market today (Feb 2026):**
- Zoko (4.9★, ~395 reviews, from $49.99/mo) — WhatsApp-first: support + cart recovery + AI sales bots
- Dondy (4.8★, ~748 reviews) — "Abandoned cart recovery, AI agent & automations on WhatsApp"
- Retainful — omnichannel email + SMS + WhatsApp, cheap ($8.99+/mo)
- Wava / CartHike — India-focused WhatsApp recovery (~₹11/message), mixed billing reviews

**Every one of them plays the same game:** recover carts *after* the customer has left.

**What nobody does — and what CartGain does:** stop the abandonment *before it happens*.

- #1 reason carts are abandoned is the price (extra costs / "too expensive" — Baymard keeps this ~48-50%)
- No recovery reminder fixes a price objection — the customer just replies STOP
- CartGain's **AI shopkeeper negotiates at checkout in real time**: lowball → counter → deal
- Whatever still abandons gets recovered via WhatsApp + Email + SMS (18–25% benchmark)

**The one-line pitch:**
> They recover carts after people leave. We turn "too expensive" into "sold" before they do — and recover what's left.

**Framing rule:** never lead with "abandoned cart". Lead with the negotiation. Cart recovery is table stakes;
the negotiation engine is the hook.

## 4. Objection handling (DMs / replies)

- **"We use Zoko/Klaviyo."** Good — they recover after the cart dies. Does any of them negotiate the price
  at checkout before the customer leaves? That's the piece we add; it also makes their messages less
  necessary. No need to rip anything out — install alongside.
- **"AI will give away my margin."** You set a hard floor; the engine physically cannot go under it.
  Try the demo and try to break it — that's the point.
- **"Customers will always haggle now."** They already do on WhatsApp and on the street. Now you control
  the outcome instead of reacting to it.
- **"Too expensive."** Free trial: first 50 recovered carts on us. We earn only when you do (revenue share).
