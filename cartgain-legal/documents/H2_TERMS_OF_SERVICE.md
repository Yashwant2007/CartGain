## 7. REVENUE-SHARE MODEL — ATTRIBUTION & AUDIT

- **Attribution:** A cart is "Recovered" only when `convertedAt` is set via **verified webhook** from Your e-commerce platform (Shopify `orders/create`, WooCommerce `order.created`) matching `cartId` within the **attribution window** (default 7 days, configurable 1–30 days).
- **No Double-Counting:** Each `cartId` generates at most one `RevenueShareEvent` (unique constraint).
- **Discount Attribution:** If AI Bargain generated a discount code used in the order, that discount amount is deducted from Gross Recovered Value before Revenue Share calculation.
- **Audit Right:** You may request a reconciliation report (CSV of `RevenueShareEvent` + webhook payloads) quarterly. We provide within 10 business days.
- **Discrepancy Resolution:** Good-faith discussion → independent auditor (split cost) if unresolved.

**10.1 Functionality.** AI Bargain negotiates prices with Customers within Merchant-defined parameters:
- **Floor Price** (minimum): Set per product or globally (`minProfitPercent` or absolute `minPrice`).

- **Max Attempts:** 1–10 per session.
- **Auto-Accept:** Disabled by default. If enabled (`autoAcceptEnabled=true`), AI may accept Customer offers ≥ Floor and generate discount code **without human review**.

**10.2 Merchant Authorization.** By enabling AI Bargain, You:
- Authorize CartGain (via OpenAI) to communicate prices/offers to Customers.
- Confirm Floor Price and Max Discount reflect Your authorized pricing.
- Accept that AI may make errors (hallucinations, currency mistakes, unauthorized promises).

**10.3 Liability Allocation.**
- **CartGain liable for:** Platform bugs causing incorrect floor enforcement, discount code generation failures, OpenAI API outages.
- **Merchant liable for:** Setting incorrect floor/max discount, enabling auto-accept without review, product/price/inventory errors, Customer disputes on negotiated price.
- **Shared:** AI hallucination resulting in unauthorized discount — Merchant bears revenue impact; CartGain assists in revocation (Shopify discount code deletion API).

**10.4 Safeguards (Technical).** CartGain implements:
- Floor price enforced in code (never below `minPrice`).
- All AI decisions logged (`BargainMessage.metadata.modelOutput`).
- Human approval gate (default).
- Kill switch: disable AI Bargain instantly per store/product.
- Conversation export for dispute resolution.
