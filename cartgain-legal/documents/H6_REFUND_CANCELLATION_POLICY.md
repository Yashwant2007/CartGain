# H.6 — CARTGAIN REFUND & CANCELLATION POLICY

> **Version:** 2026.08.15 | **Effective:** 2026-08-16
> **Incorporated by reference into ToS §13.**

---

## 1. SUBSCRIPTION FEES

| Scenario | Refund |
|---|---|
| **Free Trial** | N/A (no payment) |
| **Monthly Plan — Cancel mid-cycle** | No refund for unused days. Access continues until period end. |
| **Annual Plan — Cancel mid-term** | No refund (annual discount = commitment). |
| **Downgrade** | Prorated credit applied to next invoice (not refunded). |
| **Upgrade** | Prorated charge immediate. |
| **CartGain Service Discontinuation** | Prorated refund for unused prepaid period. |

---

## 2. REVENUE SHARE

- **Non-refundable once invoiced.** Calculated on verified Recovered Carts.
- **Dispute Window:** 14 days from invoice date. Provide evidence (Shopify order mismatch, double-count, discount error).
- **Resolution:** Good faith → correction on next invoice or credit note.

---

## 3. EXCEPTIONAL CIRCUMSTANCES

We may consider refunds (full/partial) at our sole discretion for:
- Platform outage >24 hrs in a billing cycle (SLA credit: 1 day fee per day down)
- Material feature removal without replacement (advance notice given)
- Statutory requirement (consumer protection order)

**Request:** Email billing@cart-gain.com with details. Decision within 15 business days.

---

## 4. DATA UPON CANCELLATION

- **90-day export window:** Full API access + CSV exports from dashboard.
- **After 90 days:** Data deleted per DPA Schedule 1 §9.
- **Revenue Share Liability:** Survives cancellation for carts recovered during active subscription.
